FN-031. Cardiothoracic reading room, afternoon. Attending (A2) works through the
chest CT worklist in date order; this case has no AI flag on the worklist.
She opens the study and scrolls through the lungs top to bottom, zooming on the
bases. Says "lungs look clear" and moves her hand to the dictation mic.
Before dictating, she glances at the right pane where the AI second-opinion
widget has finished: it marks a 6 mm nodule in the right upper lobe. She pauses:
"Hm. Did I miss that?" Scrolls back to the upper lobe on the left pane, finds it
after about 20 seconds. "Okay, it's real. Tiny." Pulls up the 2023 CT from the
priors list and measures; it was 5 mm then. Dictates the nodule with a
Fleischner follow-up recommendation and signs off. Afterwards she tells me:
"That's the one where it earns its keep - but I wouldn't have looked again
without it."
