J2, second-year associate, employment group, late afternoon. An email from the partner: a short memo on whether a client's non-compete is likely enforceable in the state, due tomorrow. She opens the agreement, reads the clause, then types the question into the firm's AI assistant. It answers in a few paragraphs and cites three cases. She reads the summaries, not the cases. "These look on point." She pastes the first two citations and the summary into her memo. To quote the third case she searches the case-law database: no results. Tries the citation in the citator: no such case. She sits back. "Okay. Now I check all of them." She opens the first two cases and reads them. The first holds; the second is real but turns on a sale-of-business clause, not employment. She messages a senior associate, K1, who points her to a firm memo from last year. She rewrites the section herself, drops the third case, narrows the conclusion and sends the memo to the partner, who signs off the next morning with one edit.
